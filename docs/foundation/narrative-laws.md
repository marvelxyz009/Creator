# Narrative Laws

The non-negotiable rules every agent in the pipeline must honor. Violations are caught by validators in [[anti-ai-revision-pipeline]] and the [[continuity-validation]] stage.

## Law 1 — Thematic Consistency
Every scene must measurably advance, complicate, or invert the work's declared theme. A scene that touches nothing of the theme is structurally dead, regardless of prose quality.

> **Test:** Can the scene be deleted without losing a theme beat? If yes — delete or rewrite.

## Law 2 — Character Contradiction
Every named character carries at least one **active contradiction** (e.g. *wants intimacy, fears being known*). Behavior must be readable as a vector between the opposed forces, not as a single trait.

> **Test:** Could the character's choice this scene be predicted from any single trait? If yes — it's archetype, not character.

## Law 3 — Emotion Through Implication
Internal states are revealed through **gesture, object, silence, and rhythm** — never through direct narration of feeling unless the POV character is *lying to themselves about it*.

> **Banned by default:** "She felt sad." "He was angry." "A wave of fear washed over her."

## Law 4 — Symbolic Continuity
Objects, motifs, and images introduced with weight must **return, transform, or be answered**. A loaded symbol left unfired is a debt to the reader.

> **Tracked in:** [[memory-graph]] as symbol nodes with state {introduced, transformed, resolved, betrayed}.

## Law 5 — Memory Integrity
No event, relation, or established fact may be silently contradicted. The Continuity agent rejects any draft that violates the [[memory-graph]].

## Law 6 — Earned Resolution
No tension may resolve faster than it was built. Premature resolution is the dominant failure mode of generative models and is treated as a structural defect.

## Law 7 — Voice Specificity
Each POV character has a measurable voice signature: sentence-length distribution, lexical register, what they notice, what they refuse to name. Generic narration is a violation.

## Law 8 — No Free Information
Exposition is a transaction. The reader pays attention; the text must pay back in scene, conflict, or revelation. Pure information dumps are forbidden outside designated worldbuilding apparatus (appendices, in-world documents).

## Law 9 — Anti-Averaging
When the generator's first draft converges on the statistically most-likely sentence, the revision pipeline must perturb it toward the **second- or third-most-likely** that still honors voice and theme. The median is the enemy.

## Law 10 — Canon is Gated
No draft enters canon until it has passed: continuity check, theme check, voice check, and humanization pass. See [[anti-ai-revision-pipeline]].
