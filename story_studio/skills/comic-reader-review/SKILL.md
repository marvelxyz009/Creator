# Comic reader review

Open the actual image before review. Read it in intended direction without relying on the script; identify what a reader learns. Then compare it with source-linked storyboard and references.

Check exact dialogue/diacritics, speaker attribution, panel order, visible action, identity, injury side, prop continuity, anatomy and protected expressions. Distinguish mandatory missing information from aesthetic preference. Review in thumbnail and reading size. Record concrete defects and their scene impact. Passing a file/schema validator is not a visual quality pass.

Approve only the inspected page revision and current source lineage. Route a text-only defect to lettering, composition defects to storyboard and identity defects to reference-guided art revision. Preserve successful parts and bound retries.
