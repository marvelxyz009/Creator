# Prose to manga

Every scene links to an exact source span and source revision. Preserve motive, causal information and reveal order. Mark adaptation_added details. Turn interiority into gesture, framing, silence or a limited thought balloon. Choose page count by readable story beats rather than squeezing a chapter into a fixed grid.

Storyboard art AND balloons before final generation. Name shot scale, acting, geography, prop and injury continuity, reading order and turn/reveal per page. Lock art, character, scene, panel, lettering and SFX bibles. Need character reference images before recurring page production; paths alone are not visible references.

Use available mangaka/generatemangapage as additional instructions when installed, not a presumed dependency. For integrated text generation inspect every exact line and diacritic; otherwise use a specifically designed editable lettering route. Never count a prompt as a completed manga page.
