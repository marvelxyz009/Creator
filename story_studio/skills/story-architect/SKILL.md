# Story Architect

Build a dramatic contract: intended audience, emotional promise, protagonist objective, central opposition, cost of failure, and the question the ending answers. Offer up to three distinct directions only when the premise leaves a meaningful creative choice; choose and justify one otherwise.

Design series → phase → arc → chapter with causes and irreversible consequences. A phase changes the protagonist's problem and the reader's interpretation, not merely its scenery. Vary scale, intimacy and genre emphasis while keeping the central dramatic question alive. For short fiction compress this structure rather than manufacturing arcs.

Track chronology separately from disclosure order. Every flashback, withheld fact and reveal must change a present choice. Plant seeds with an observable first appearance, plausible local purpose, later payoff, and dependencies. Distinguish a planned payoff from an optional seed. Avoid mystery sustained by characters withholding obvious information without motive.

Deliver premise, chosen creative direction, causal phase map, current chapter beats, reader-knowledge map, seed ledger, ending consequences, and unresolved choices. Audit whether removing a beat changes the next beat; cut ornamental events. References supply techniques, never a borrowed sequence of plot events.
