## What the chapter does that no single scene could

1. **Symmetry inversion via POV shift.** Mira's body-knowledge in scene 01 had no archival record. Tomas's conscience-knowledge in scene 03 has no archival record either. The chapter constructs this parallel structurally, not through any character naming it.
2. **Debt rebalancing.** The chapter starts with debt on Tomas and self-debt on Mira; ends with two debts on Tomas and Mira's self-debt retired-by-counter-move. This rebalancing IS the chapter's plot.
3. **Time pressure introduction.** Rule r2 (the forty-day window) was a static world fact at chapter open. By chapter close it is an active debt with a date.
4. **POV shift legibility.** The reader gains Tomas's interior precisely at the moment his interior gains complexity. The shift is structurally efficient because it lands on the chapter's invert beat.
