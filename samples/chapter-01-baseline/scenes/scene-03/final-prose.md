# Scene 03 — Final Prose (canonized)

> **POV shift:** first interior-Tomas scene of the work.
> **Function in chapter:** end-turn. Closes Chapter 01.
> Word count: ~600.

---

The lighter was in his palm before he had decided to pick it up.

He noticed it as he set the kettle on, the way one notices a sound that has been there for some time before one names it. He put the lighter on the table. He turned the kettle off because he had not, he saw, actually turned it on. He turned it on. The window above the sink was the kind of cold light he had stopped paying attention to. The harbor was visible. The bell had not yet rung.

The wall calendar's bottom edge had drifted a degree out of true. He fixed it. The coat on the back of the chair had slid half an inch on one side. He fixed it. The picture frame on the table edge — small, brass, no picture inside it yet — sat a finger's breadth too close to the edge. He fixed that too.

The Archive icon was on his terminal. He had not opened the terminal. The icon was the kind that pushed itself.

He read it standing.

Halren, Mira J. — a date and a time, last night, the contact log of a name he did not recognize but did not need to. The survivor registry. He read the line twice and then he closed it without acting and the screen took a moment to register that he had closed it.

The date in the corner of the screen was a date. He read it. He did not register it. He registered it.

The second picture frame on the shelf above the desk was also a finger's breadth too close. He fixed it. He registered, as his hand was straightening it, that he had fixed two frames before the notification and two after, and that his hand had decided to fix the second one without telling him.

He stopped.

The lighter was in his palm again. He did not remember crossing back to the table. He turned it once. He set it on the windowsill. He set it at the angle he had set it at the consultation desk the night before. He did not know why he did this. He did not narrate it to himself. He looked at it from one degree off true and he straightened it.

He thought, *partly*. He thought, *within reason*. He did not finish the third clause.

He walked away from the window.

The lighter remained on the sill.
